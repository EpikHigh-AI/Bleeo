Start with a calmer feed

Open a supported site, then click the Bleeo toolbar icon. Medium sensitivity is a good starting point. Click softened text to reveal it for 9 seconds, or focus it and press Enter or Space.

Where does Bleeo work?

CNN, BBC.com, The New York Times, Fox News, New York Post, The Guardian, The Washington Post, Google News, Reddit, X / Twitter, YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, Threads.net, TikTok, and LinkedIn, including their subdomains.

Other sites and browser settings pages are outside the filter's scope. Detection depends on the site's layout and currently targets English wording.

Your text stays on your device

All filtering happens locally in the browser. Bleeo does not send article or feed text to a server.

General preferences can sync through your browser account. Site preferences and pauses stay in this browser profile.
